
# Case Study 33: Online Portfolio Builder

## 1. Problem Statement

Students need a platform to showcase their projects, technical skills, certificates, and achievements. The Online Portfolio Builder helps students create and share professional portfolios.

## 2. Product Backlog

| ID | User Story | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| PBI-1 | As a student, I want to register and log in. | High |
| PBI-2 | As a student, I want to create my profile. | High |
| PBI-3 | As a student, I want to add skills and achievements. | High |
| PBI-4 | As a student, I want to upload project details. | High |
| PBI-5 | As a student, I want to add certificates. | Medium |
| PBI-6 | As a student, I want to share my portfolio link. | Medium |
| PBI-7 | As a student, I want to customize my portfolio. | Low |

## 3. Sprint Plan

| Sprint | Duration | Planned Features | Sprint Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sprint 1 | 1 week | Registration, login, student profile, skills | Students can create their profiles. |
| Sprint 2 | 1 week | Projects, certificates, customization, sharing | Students can create and share portfolios. |

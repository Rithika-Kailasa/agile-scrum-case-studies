
# Case Study 31: Food Wastage Reduction App

## 1. Problem Statement

Restaurants often have excess food, while NGOs need food to support people in need. This application connects restaurants with NGOs so that surplus food can be donated instead of wasted.

## 2. Product Backlog

| ID | User Story | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| PBI-1 | As a restaurant, I want to register and log in. | High |
| PBI-2 | As a restaurant, I want to list excess food. | High |
| PBI-3 | As an NGO, I want to view available donations. | High |
| PBI-4 | As an NGO, I want to request food donations. | High |
| PBI-5 | As a restaurant, I want to confirm donation requests. | Medium |
| PBI-6 | As an NGO, I want to track food collection. | Medium |
| PBI-7 | As a user, I want to receive donation notifications. | Low |

## 3. Sprint Plan

| Sprint | Duration | Planned Features | Sprint Goal |
|---|---|---|---|

| Sprint 2 | 1 week | Donation requests, confirmation, collection tracking | NGOs can request donations and track collection. |
